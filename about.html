01OUR BEGINNING

Responding to hardship with support that respects the person.

Established in 2021, Al Shehza Foundation began with a commitment to help people and families facing poverty and hardship. Its work focuses on practical needs that affect daily life: access to water, food, children’s nourishment, and resources for learning.

The foundation’s programmes include shared water facilities at mosques and madrasas, household tube wells, rice and iftar food support, yoghurt donations for children, Qurban meat distribution, and Quran donations for children’s studies.
