The reason behind the work

A helping hand should meet a real need—and always see the person.

Al Shehza Foundation (Guarantee) Limited was established in 2021 as a non-profit committed to uplifting underserved communities, easing poverty, and restoring human dignity through compassionate, structured social work.

We listen to the needs people share and respond with thoughtful, useful support. Some needs call for immediate relief; others ask for steady access to essentials that can make daily life more secure.

“Care is not just what we give. It is the respect with which we give it.”
