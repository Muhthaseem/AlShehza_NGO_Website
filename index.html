01OUR PURPOSE

Meeting essential needs with care, respect, and community.

Established in 2021, the foundation works to ease poverty and hardship through practical community initiatives. Its programmes bring water closer to households and shared spaces, provide food support, and make nourishment and learning resources available to children.
